The covariance equals p₀ − pMpC, where p₀ is the probability of no openings and pM, pC are the probabilities of missing the two edge classes. Three freezes are needed to force no opening. One freeze suffices in each displayed miss history. Thus p₀ is of order α³, while pMpC has a lower bound of order α². The paper makes the comparison strict for 0 < α ≤ 1/1000.
