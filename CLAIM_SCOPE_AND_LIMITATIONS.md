# Claim scope and limitations

- The final open-edge law in the finite, homogeneous constant-freezing model can violate positive association. The analytic witness is $K_4$ and the stated range is $0<\alpha\leq 1/1000$.
- For the fixed perfect matching and complementary edge class, the exact formula determines the covariance at every positive rate. Above its positive root, positive covariance for this pair leaves association of the full law undetermined.
- Graph-size minimality, the set of all rates admitting some counterexample, and association for an infinite-graph law remain outside the result.
- Mottram’s four-vertex path supplies a calibration for the model and its lattice defect. It is positively associated.
- The analytic result has a self-contained proof. The all-rate formula uses an executable exact-arithmetic certificate with a proved degree bound.
