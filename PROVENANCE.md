# Provenance

The cited problem is Mottram’s positive-association question in §3.1.4 of his dissertation, printed pp. 43–44. The same question is indexed as Q277 in the Open Problems Catalog at revision `ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe`. The model follows Definition 3.2.1 and the finite definition in Mottram’s journal-paper preprint.

The present paper uses a discovery proof built from three jump histories on $K_4$, an earlier AI-assisted manuscript and exact-rate appendix, and an independent exact forward-flow check. The current certificate verifies a rational identity at 63 distinct rational nodes against an established degree bound. The illustration uses the same three histories as the analytic proof.
